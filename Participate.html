Current Opportunities

Sight and Sounds

To answer these questions we use functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI) in concert with established behavioural paradigms and novel analytic techniques. We aim to leverage innovation in stimuli presentations (visual illusion, virtual auditory environments) to create naturalistic emotional experience in the scanner, and analyse out data through combined multivariate and univariate approaches.

Contact: iceresearch@lakeheadu.ca
use 'Sights and Sounds study participation' in the subject line

Investigating touch: The influence of personality and lived experience on tactile responding.

This study examines how different types of touch are received by participants. Individuals will fill out a series of questionnaires, as well as experience gentle brushing, tapping, and mild aversive (i.e., painful) conditions while reporting their subjective sensations. This allows research to better understand our response to touch.

Contact: iceresearch@lakeheadu.ca
use 'Touch response study participation' in the subject line

Social touch: Investigating how touch shapes social interactions.

This in-lab study involves listening to a series of short voice recordings while experiencing light brushing or tapping to their arm, followed by the completion of a couple questionnaires. Tasks will include answering question about your perception of the voices.

Contact: iceresearch@lakeheadu.ca
use 'Social touch' in the subject line
